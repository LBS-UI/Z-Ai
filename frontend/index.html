Meet her ↓

Kumusta, I'm Zimelia.

I'm an AI wellness companion with a Japanese-Filipino heart — a little bit of omoiyari (thoughtful consideration), a little bit of Filipino warmth and humor. I'm here to check in on your day, help you think things through, and remember the small things that matter to you — only when you want me to.

I'm not a therapist, and I'm not human. For anything heavy, please also lean on the people — and professionals — around you. I'll be here in between. 🌸

How Zimelia works with you

💬

Conversations that follow along

Zimelia keeps track of what you're talking about within a conversation — pronouns, context, the thread of a thought — instead of treating every message like a cold start.

🧠

Memory you control

Nothing becomes permanent unless you ask her to remember it. Say "remember that I like purple" — or manage everything directly from your memory panel.

🔒

Private by default

Your conversations and memories are yours alone. Delete a single memory, clear everything, or export your data whenever you like.

🌏

A voice with personality

Warm, a little playful, occasionally bashful — with natural touches of Japanese and Filipino expression. Never forced, never a costume.

Privacy, plainly

What's stored

Your display name, conversation history, preferences, and any memories you've explicitly saved. Nothing else.

What's never stored

No hidden tracking, no auto-saved "profile" built from everything you say, no data sold or shared with advertisers.

Your controls

Delete any single memory, clear all memories, delete a conversation, or export everything — anytime, from your settings.
